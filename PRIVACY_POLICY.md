# Privacy Policy for ClassMode
**Effective Date:** September 24, 2026

## 1. Introduction
ClassMode ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. We built this application with a strict "Offline-First" and "Privacy-First" mandate. This policy explains how your information is handled when you use the ClassMode Android application.

## 2. Offline-First Guarantee & Local Data
ClassMode does **not** require a user account. All core functional data—including your class timetables, automation rules, custom focus sessions, and location waypoints (geofences)—are stored strictly locally in your device's internal database (Room/DataStore). **We do not collect, transmit, sync, or sell your personal schedule or location data to any external server.**

## 3. Permissions We Request and Why
To function autonomously, ClassMode requests the following Android system permissions:
*   **Do Not Disturb (Notification Policy Access):** Required to automatically switch your device to Silent or Do Not Disturb mode.
*   **Location (Foreground & Background):** Required *only* if you enable the optional Campus Geofencing feature. This data never leaves your device and is processed locally by Android OS to trigger rules.
*   **Alarms & Reminders:** Required for precise class start/end triggers.

## 4. Third-Party Services
While ClassMode operates offline, it integrates specific third-party SDKs that may collect data if network access is available:

*   **Google AdMob:** We use AdMob to serve non-intrusive banner ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers (like the Android Advertising ID), IP addresses, and generalized location data to serve relevant ads. We strictly configure these ads not to interrupt active class or focus sessions.
*   **Firebase Crashlytics (Opt-In Only):** We use Crashlytics to monitor app stability. **This feature is disabled by default.** If you explicitly opt-in via Settings, Crashlytics will collect anonymous crash traces, device models, and OS versions to help us fix bugs.

## 5. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be reflected in this document with an updated "Effective Date."
