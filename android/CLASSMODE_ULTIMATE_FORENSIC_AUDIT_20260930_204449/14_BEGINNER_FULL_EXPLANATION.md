# FULL EXPLANATION (PLAIN ENGLISH)

ClassMode is an app that changes your phone's volume automatically based on time or location. 

Right now, it has a beautiful interface but serious internal confusion. If you tell the app to be 'Silent' at 10 AM, the app actually ignores your choice and makes it 'Vibrate' instead because of a hardcoded rule. If you turn off a schedule, it works until you restart your phone, at which point the app forgets you turned it off and turns it back on. Also, the Bengali language translation is completely corrupted.
